FAQ

Good to know

How much does it cost to hire the ground?

Pricing depends on the date, time, length of hire and what you need (floodlights, changing rooms, hospitality). Send an enquiry with your details and we'll give you an exact price.

Can we book a regular weekly slot?

Yes — many teams book recurring training or match slots. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll sort out a regular arrangement.

Do you have floodlights for evening games?

Yes. The pitch is floodlit, so evening and winter fixtures and training are no problem.

Is there parking and are spectators welcome?

There's free on-site parking and a spectator area, so families and supporters are very welcome.

What formats can we play?

The full-size pitch suits 11-a-side, and can be set up for 5, 7 and 9-a-side too. Let us know your format in the enquiry.
