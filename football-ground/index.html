Welcome to Coal Park Lane

The home of grassroots football.

From under-7s mini-soccer right up to 11-a-side, Coal Park Lane is a home for grassroots football. Junior clubs, youth teams, academies and school sides train and play here on 5, 7, 9 and 11-a-side pitches — with a kitchen for half-time snacks and match-day teas, and plenty of free parking for the parents. And it's not just football — the site also hosts wedding receptions, kids' parties and events. Whatever the occasion, enquire with your preferred date and we'll confirm availability and pricing straight back to you.

FAQ

Good to know

How much does it cost to hire a pitch?

Pricing depends on which pitch, the date, time and how long you need it. Send an enquiry with your details and we'll give you an exact price.

Can we book a regular weekly slot?

Yes — lots of teams book recurring 5-a-side or training slots. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll set up a regular arrangement.

Can you do food for a league night or party?

Our on-site kitchen holds a 5-star food hygiene rating and can provide hot food and refreshments. Let us know what you need in your enquiry.

Is there parking?

Yes — there's free on-site parking for players and visitors, and CCTV in operation on site.

What pitches do you have?

Three 5-a-side pitches, three 7-a-side pitches and a full-size pitch marked for both 9-a-side and 11-a-side — so we can host everything from under-7s mini-soccer right up to 11-a-side.

Can we hold a wedding reception or event here?

Yes — the site works well for wedding receptions, functions and outdoor events. We have large single-phase and three-phase external power ready for marquees, caterers, bars and staging, plus the on-site kitchen, free parking and CCTV. Tell us what you're planning in your enquiry.
