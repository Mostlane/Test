🔗 SiteLog ↔ Portal links

📊 Job Costing

Link each SiteLog scanning person to their portal user. Once linked, that person is treated as an employee in job costing — their labour is priced at the portal rate and fuel/travel allowances (from Engineer Timesheet settings), and their SiteLog scans tie into the portal timesheet. People left unlinked are costed on SiteLog's own rate + fuel (fine for subcontractors). One portal user links to one SiteLog person.
