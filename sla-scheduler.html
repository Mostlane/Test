🛟 Fallback jobs

Each field engineer can have a fallback job — somewhere to head if nothing else is booked. The system warns the office at 3:30pm & 6pm about anyone with no job for the next working day, then auto-assigns their fallback at 7pm (Friday covers Monday). Fallback jobs appear to the engineer at 5pm the evening before, and are dropped automatically if they get a real job that day.
