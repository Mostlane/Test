🔄 Sync with Workever

Bring the portal in line with Workever — update jobs that are done there but still showing as pending here, and pull in any job that's missing from your records. Runs from a snippet you paste into your logged-in Workever tab (Workever has no API key, so the fetch has to happen there).

1 · Status mapping

How each Workever status maps into the portal, and which ones count as done (a live job is only ever closed, never re-opened). Defaults are set — adjust if needed.

Loading…
2 · Test one job first (recommended)

Enter the MOS number (or job id) of a job you know is done in Workever but still Pending here. It syncs just that one job and reports whether its notes, signature and photos all arrived — a clear ❌ if anything Workever holds fails to transfer. Paste the runner into the Workever console like the full run.

3 · Run the full sync
  1. Open mostlane.workever.app in another tab and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Press the button below to copy the runner, then in that Workever tab open the console (F12 → Console), paste, and press Enter.
  3. Leave it running — it pages through every job and reports progress; it's safe to stop and re-run (it resumes).
4 · What changed

Brings across job records (status, description, cost, site) and their photos, signatures & documents — but only for jobs it actually syncs; a job you completed in the portal is never touched, and files already in your archive are skipped. Photos stream in the background, so the file count may keep rising for a moment after the pages finish.
