Verify stored certificates

Runs the EICR verifier against every stored 5-year (EICR) certificate. A cert is marked ⚠ needs attention (red) only when it's genuinely actionable — stated Unsatisfactory, a mis-coded outcome (marked Satisfactory but carrying a C1/C2/FI), or a cert that couldn't be read. Softer findings (signatures, LIM, cable/Zs notes) and C3 recommendations show on each row as 💡 notes, not red. A site is skipped if no document has been added since its last check. Best run on a desktop with the tab open; it's resumable. The certificate never leaves your device.
