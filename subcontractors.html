The routing list for sending a job out to a subcontractor (Metro Rod and others). Set where each one's order email goes, which job details it carries, the work areas they cover, their coverage area (base postcode + radius) and whether they do out-of-hours — so the job card can suggest the right one and the portal can nudge you out-of-hours.

You can view the setup but only a Full Access user can change it.

📤 Orders are sent from

The Microsoft 365 mailbox every subcontractor order goes out from. It shows as the sender, keeps a copy in that mailbox's Sent Items, and replies come back to it. If Microsoft 365 can't send, the portal sends it from its own address instead (with replies still to this mailbox) and the job card says so.

🗺️ Coverage map

Each subcontractor with a base postcode + radius shows its coverage bubble, so you can see who reaches where.
