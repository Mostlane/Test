Customer status emails

How it works

When a job's status changes, the portal can automatically email the customer a branded update including the job details. Scheduling emails carry a notice asking them to email enquiries@mostlane.com as soon as possible if the time isn't convenient, to avoid a cancellation charge. Emails go to the site contact's email, or the customer's if the site has none.
